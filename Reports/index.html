Four exploratory clustering reports

All reports use the nine-vowel extraction and the shared sample of 83 complete villages. Compare constraints explicitly: geographic coherence is partly imposed when Gabriel adjacency is used.

1. Whole vowel configurations

Ward clustering of all nine vowels together, comparing PCA and FastTrack Bark, 50% alone versus all timepoints, and 2–6 clusters. Includes maps, cluster means and speaker-bootstrap stability.

2. Independent-vowel profiles

Cluster each vowel independently, then exact nine-cell profiles and nested groups sharing 8–3 common vowel cells. No geographic constraint at either stage.

3. Gabriel vowel clusters only

Geographically connected Ward clusters per vowel. Exact row equality and later profile merges remain unconstrained.

4. Gabriel at both stages

Constrain both individual-vowel clusters and profile merges. Identical rows are first split into connected components; shared-core groups remain geographically connected.

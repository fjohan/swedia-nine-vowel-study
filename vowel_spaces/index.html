Method

Coordinate-wise token medians within each speaker and vowel, then medians across speakers within each village. The two-standard-deviation covariance ellipse uses all available vowel anchors, including unpaired /iː/. The six original directed vowel-pair arrows remain; no midpoint markers or midpoint ellipse. Incomplete inventories, Gotland and Finland are retained. PCA uses the new nine-vowel model, displayed as −PC1/−PC2; formants use −Bark F2/−Bark F1. Angles are signed from the horizontal (−90° to +90°); near-circular ellipses are marked unstable. These are descriptive ellipses, not confidence intervals.

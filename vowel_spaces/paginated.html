Nine-vowel village spaces

Token medians are calculated separately for each speaker, vowel and temporal point; village anchors are then coordinate-wise medians of the speaker medians. Each available vowel contributes one anchor to the covariance ellipse, including unpaired /iː/. Speakers and villages with incomplete inventories are retained; panel titles give the number of vowels available.

The ellipse is drawn at two covariance standard deviations, not as a confidence interval. Its major-axis angle is measured from the displayed horizontal (signed −90° to +90°); nearly circular ellipses are labelled unstable. The six original directed vowel pairs are retained, with no midpoint markers or midpoint ellipse. /iː/ has no arrows. Gotland and Finland are included.

PCA uses the newly fitted nine-vowel model, displayed as −PC1 and −PC2. FastTrack uses −Bark F2 and −Bark F1. No geographic model is fitted.
